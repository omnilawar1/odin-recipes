Cheesecake

A velvety Madagascar vanilla bean cream cheese meticulously set atop a rich, brown butter crust, celebrating pure, elegant dessert simplicity.

Ingredients

  • Graham Crackers: 200g (finely crushed)
  • Unsalted Butter: 80g (melted)
  • Sugar: 1 tablespoon (granulated)
  • Full-Fat Cream Cheese: 500g (softened to room temperature)
  • Granulated Sugar: 150g (caster sugar is best for a smooth texture)
  • Heavy Cream or Sour Cream: 200ml
  • Cornstarch: 2 tablespoons
  • Vanilla Extract: 1.5 teaspoons
  • Lemon Juice: 1 teaspoon (or zest of half a lemon to brighten the flavor)
  • Salt: A small pinch

Steps

  1. Prepare the Crust: Finely crush the biscuits and combine them with melted butter and the tablespoon of sugar. Press this sandy mixture firmly and evenly into the base of a lined springform pan, then refrigerate while preparing the filling.
  2. Cream the Base: In a large bowl, whisk the softened cream cheese and 150g of sugar until the mixture is perfectly smooth and free of any lumps.
  3. Incorporate Dry Ingredients: Sift in the cornstarch and the pinch of salt, mixing gently until just combined to ensure the structure remains stable without adding excess air.
  4. Finish the Batter: Gradually fold in the heavy cream, vanilla extract, and lemon juice until the consistency is velvety and uniform.
  5. Bake: Pour the batter over the prepared crust. Bake at 160°C (320°F) for 45–50 minutes; the edges should appear set and slightly puffed, while the center retains a delicate, gentle wobble.
  6. Cool and Set: Turn off the oven, leaving the door slightly ajar for one hour to allow for gradual cooling—this prevents cracking. Once cool, refrigerate for at least 6 hours to achieve a firm, elegant slice.
