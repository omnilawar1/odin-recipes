Thai Curry

An aromatic infusion of garden-harvested vegetables, gently poached in a velvet coconut emulsion, perfectly balanced with hand-pounded, authentic Thai aromatics.

Ingredients

  • Green Curry Paste: 3 tablespoons (store-bought or homemade)
  • Coconut Milk: 400ml (full-fat for a rich, silky texture)
  • Broccoli: 1 small head (cut into bite-sized florets)
  • Zucchini: 1 medium (sliced into half-moons)
  • Red Bell Pepper: 1 large (sliced thinly for a sweet crunch)
  • Firm Tofu: 200g (pressed and cubed to soak up the broth)
  • Vegetable Broth: 120ml
  • Soy Sauce: 1.5 tablespoons (or tamari for a gluten-free option)
  • Brown Sugar: 1 teaspoon (to balance the aromatic heat)
  • Thai Basil Leaves: A generous handful (freshly torn)

Steps

  1. Awaken the Aromatics: Heat a splash of oil in a large wok or pot over medium heat. Add the green curry paste and sauté for 2 minutes until intensely fragrant and the oils begin to gently separate.
  2. Create the Base: Pour in the creamy coconut milk and vegetable broth, whisking smoothly until the curry paste dissolves completely into a beautiful, pale green liquid. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Simmer the Hearty Vegetables: Toss the broccoli florets, zucchini half-moons, and red bell pepper slices into the bubbling broth. Let it simmer softly for 5-7 minutes until the vegetables are tender yet retain a crisp, bright bite.
  4. Add the Delicate Ingredients: Gently fold in the cubed tofu and allow it to cook for 2-3 minutes, gently warming through and absorbing the rich, complex flavors of the coconut broth.
  5. Balance the Flavors: Stir in the soy sauce and brown sugar. Taste the curry and adjust the seasoning to ensure a perfect, elegant harmony of spicy, savory, and subtly sweet notes.
  6. Finish and Serve: Turn off the heat and fold in the fresh Thai basil leaves until just wilted. Serve immediately, ladling the vibrant curry over a steaming bowl of fragrant jasmine rice.
