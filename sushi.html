Sushi

Velvety avocado encased in vinegared sushi rice and crisp nori; a refined, plant-forward composition balancing delicate textures and pure flavor.

Ingredients

  • Sushi Rice: 200g (short-grain)
  • Water: 240ml (cold)
  • Rice Vinegar: 3 tablespoons (seasoned)
  • Sugar: 1 tablespoon (granulated)
  • Salt: 1/2 teaspoon
  • Nori Sheets: 2 full sheets (crisp and roasted)
  • Premium Bluefin Tuna: 150g (sushi-grade, sliced into strips)
  • Cucumber: 1/2 small (julienned to add a crisp texture)
  • Soy Sauce: A small dash (for dipping)
  • Wasabi: A small pinch

Steps

  1. Prepare the Rice: Rinse the short-grain rice under cold water until it runs clear, then cook with 240ml of water. Let it steam for 10 minutes to ensure a perfectly sticky foundation.
  2. Season the Base: Gently fold the seasoned rice vinegar, sugar, and salt into the warm rice using a wooden spatula, slicing through to ensure it remains fluffy without mashing the grains.
  3. Cool and Cover: Spread the seasoned rice onto a large tray to cool to room temperature, keeping it covered with a damp cloth to prevent the grains from drying out.
  4. Assemble the Roll: Lay a crisp nori sheet shiny side down on a bamboo mat. Wet your hands and press an even, thin layer of rice over the nori, leaving a small border at the top edge.
  5. Add Fillings: Arrange the strips of premium bluefin tuna and julienned cucumber horizontally across the lower third of the rice-covered nori.
  6. Roll and Slice: Lift the bottom of the bamboo mat and roll tightly over the fillings. Squeeze gently to shape, then use a sharp, wet knife to slice the roll into eight elegant, bite-sized pieces.
